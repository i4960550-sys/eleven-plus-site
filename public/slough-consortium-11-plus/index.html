The Slough Consortium 11+: change of assessment provider from 2027

The Slough Consortium — Herschel, Langley, St Bernard's Catholic and Upton Court grammar schools — is changing the provider of its 11+ assessment. From the September 2027 sitting, the assessment is provided by Quest Assessments, replacing GL Assessment.

Who this applies to

Children currently in year 5 sit the Quest Assessments 11+ in September 2027, for entry in September 2028. Children currently in Year 4 sit this in September 2028, for entry in September 2029.

Format

Two papers, each around 50 minutes:

  • Paper 1 — English comprehension and verbal reasoning
  • Paper 2 — Non-verbal reasoning and maths

Where to check

Quest Assessments publishes free familiarisation material for the Consortium assessment: a familiarisation booklet for each of the four subjects, guidance written for children, a vocabulary list and recommended reading lists.

  • Quest Assessments, Slough Consortium 11+ assessment
  • The four schools' admissions pages, for the arrangements confirmed each year.

We publish practice sets designed around the Quest format for the Slough Consortium 11+ — ten sets, each with Paper 1 and Paper 2, answer keys and a written explanation for every question.
